Research Objective & Data Foundation

Predicting national economic growth (Real GDP per Capita) across diverse global economies is notoriously difficult. Policy-makers and economists need accurate growth projections to plan sovereign debt, infrastructure, and fiscal policy.

Traditional econometric models (like simple Auto-Regressive models) are good at capturing long-term steady trends, but miss sudden non-linear shifts. Conversely, a stacked Ensemble of Machine Learning (ML) models—combining LightGBM gradient boosting, Ridge regression, CatBoost, and XGBoost—can capture non-linear cross-country macroeconomic interactions.

Data Source Overview: Geo-Macroeconomic Dataset (GMD v6)

The research is powered by the Geo-Macroeconomic Dataset (GMD v6, Müller et al., 2026), providing 15,071 country-year observations (12,166 valid labeled target rows) across 237 sovereign nations and territories from 1960 to 2024.

Key Empirical Findings & Benchmark Results

Finding 1: ML Ensemble Dominates Short-Term, Classic Econometrics Dominates Long-Term

A major breakthrough of this project is demonstrating scientific honesty. We do not claim machine learning beats everything everywhere. Instead, our 5-fold walk-forward cross-validation reveals a clear structural crossover:

Forecast Horizon ($h$) ML Ensemble MAE (Lower is better) Classic AR(1) Model MAE Naive Persistence MAE Winning Model Class
1 Year ($h=1$) 0.0334 0.0359 0.0469 ML Ensemble (+7.0% over AR(1))
3 Years ($h=3$) 0.0695 0.0710 0.0783 ML Ensemble (+2.1% over AR(1))
5 Years ($h=5$) 0.0843 0.0686 0.0735 Classic AR(1) (+22.9% over ML)

*MAE = Mean Absolute Error (lower score means higher predictive accuracy). Evaluated via 5-fold rolling-origin walk-forward cross-validation.

Finding 2: "Historical Twins" Vector Retrieval

Using mathematical vector search (FAISS), the system searches 64 years of global history (15,071 country-years) to find historical country-years matching a target country's current macroeconomic profile.

It identifies "Divergent Twins"—countries that started in identical positions but diverged due to structural policy choices.

Finding 3: Calibrated Conformal Risk Bands

Rather than providing a single point forecast, the system calibrates non-parametric 80% prediction intervals.

It incorporates an explicit +75% lower-tail expansion to guard against severe downside macro shocks (such as pandemic crises or sovereign debt defaults).

System Architecture & Core Processing Pipeline

  • Data Harmonization & Forecast Stripping

    Standardizes 162 macro indicators across 237 countries. Strips IMF-style forecast_* rows so models train 100% on real historical observations, and removes regional aggregates (e.g. Arab World, High Income) to evaluate genuine sovereign states.

  • Multi-Horizon ML Stacking Ensemble

    Combines four complementary machine learning algorithms (LightGBM gradient boosting, Ridge linear regression, CatBoost, and XGBoost) to predict growth at 1, 3, 5, and 10-year horizons.

  • Historical Analog Search (Vector Retrieval)

    Transforms country profiles into mathematical vectors. Uses Euclidean distance over rank-transformed features to instantly retrieve historical analog situations.

  • Conformal Risk Calibration & Tail Buffer

    Instead of giving just one point prediction, the system creates calibrated 80% confidence bands with a +75% lower-tail buffer to guard against downside crisis events.

Methodological Rigor & Anti-Data-Leakage Rules

Many machine learning papers suffer from "data leakage" (accidentally letting future data influence past predictions), resulting in inflated accuracy scores. This codebase enforces six strict anti-leakage safeguards:

1. Rolling-Origin Backtest:

Models only train on years $\le t$ when evaluating year $t+1$.

2. Nested Optuna Tuning:

ML hyperparameters are tuned strictly inside historical fold windows.

3. Out-of-Time Imputation:

Missing values are filled using only historical statistics.

4. Honest AR(1) Baseline:

Evaluated on the exact same folds for an unbiased benchmark comparison.

5. No Country Memorization:

Country dummies are excluded during cross-validation to test true cross-sectional transferability.

6. 100% Test Pass Rate:

Full pytest suite covering data integrity and model inference contract.

Key Research Differentiator

While existing literature focuses almost exclusively on short-term ($h=1$) forecasts for small OECD panels (~20–30 countries), our work evaluates a massive global panel of 237 economies across multi-year horizons ($h \in \{1, 3, 5, 10\}$), discovering the structural crossover where classic economics dominates long-term forecasting.
